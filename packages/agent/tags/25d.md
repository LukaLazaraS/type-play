## Tag: 2.5D (a 3D-drawn world, played on a flat plane)

- **The world is 3D, the play is flat**: a `Node3D` scene with models from the library, light and a
  sky (the 3D rules above), but everything that moves stays on one plane and the player moves on
  two axes only. Characters, enemies and pickups are 3D models, never sprites.
- **A fixed camera, never moved by the player**: either **side-on** (a `Camera3D` looking along
  -Z, moving right and left with the hero, a little above and a little ahead; move on X and Y,
  lock Z), or **isometric / three-quarter** (a fixed angle of ~45° turned and ~35° down, an
  orthographic camera or a narrow field of view; move on X and Z, lock Y). Pick the one that fits
  the genre and the idea; keep it all game long.
- **Controls follow the screen, not the camera**: touch has the left stick (or swipe or tap) and
  the action buttons (the rules above); keys are WASD or the arrows and Space. "Right" is always
  right on the screen, also in the isometric view (turn the input by the camera's angle).
- **Depth for the look**: parallax backgrounds, props in front of and behind the plane, soft
  shadows under things, a little tilt of the model when it turns. Nothing blocks the view of the
  plane.
- **Pitfalls**: models that sink into or float over the plane (set their height once); a camera
  that shows the edge of the world (limit it); depth sorting in the isometric view (give things
  distinct heights).
- **Keep it light**: the 3D rules' limits; the check renders in software.
- **Test**: the hero moves on both axes and never leaves the plane (its locked axis stays put);
  the camera follows and stays at its fixed angle.
