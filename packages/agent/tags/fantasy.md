## Tag: Fantasy (knights, magic, dragons and castles)

- **The world is fantasy** whatever the genre: castles, enchanted forests, villages and dungeons;
  heroes are knights, wizards or elves; enemies are goblins, slimes, skeletons or dragons; coins
  are gold, gems or potions; power-ups are spells and enchanted items.
- **The look**: rich storybook colours (deep greens, royal blues, gold), stone, wood and banners,
  glowing runes and magic sparkles; titles in a bold serif-like lettering drawn in code or a
  chunky label.
- **The sound**: chimes and harps for magic, metal clangs for swords, a horn for a win.
- **In 3D**: `pack castle-kit`, `pack fantasy-town-kit`, `pack modular-dungeon-kit`, `pack
  medieval-village-mega-kit`, `pack knight-character-animated` or `pack goblin-animated`.
