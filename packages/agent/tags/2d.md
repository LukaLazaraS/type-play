## Tag: 2D (flat, drawn in code)

- Make the game 2D even where the genre or the idea suggests 3D: a `Node2D` scene, every
  character, piece and place drawn in code (the 2D art rules above), a `Camera2D` if the view moves.
- No 3D nodes and no models from the 3D library. Depth comes from the drawing: layers, parallax,
  shadows under things, a side or top-down view.
