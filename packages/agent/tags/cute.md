## Tag: Cute (round, friendly characters with big eyes)

- **Everything looks friendly**: round, chubby shapes with soft corners, big shiny eyes (a white
  dot of light in each), small smiles and pink cheeks. Enemies and obstacles are cute too (a grumpy
  cloud, a sleepy rock, a pouting car), never scary or gory.
- **Soft, bright colours**: pastel bodies with a darker outline of the same hue, a light sky or
  background, a cheerful palette.
- **Bouncy motion**: squash and stretch on every jump, landing and tap; an idle wobble or blink;
  hearts, stars and sparkles as the effects; happy little sounds.
- **In 3D**: round, toy-like models (`pack cube-pets`, `pack mini-characters`, or the cutest ones
  in the idea's theme) with the same bouncy motion.
