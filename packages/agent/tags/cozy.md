## Tag: Cozy (calm and kind: no rush, no losing)

- **No losing**: no game over and no lives. A mistake costs little and gently: the runner slows
  down, a piece slides back, a lap takes longer, an enemy that gets through only shrinks the
  reward. A round ends when its goal is reached (or the player stops), with a warm summary of
  what they did.
- **No rush**: no countdowns and no move limits; a slow, steady pace that rises only a little, so
  the player can take their time.
- **A warm, soft look**: rounded shapes, warm muted colours, gentle light (a morning, a sunset,
  lamplight), small details that make the place feel lived in.
- **Kind feedback**: soft pops and sparkles, little rewards that add up (a growing collection, a
  garden, stars), calm sounds (soft chimes, no harsh buzzers, no screen shake).
- Where the genre needs a threat (tower defence), keep it mild: friendly critters to turn away
  rather than monsters to kill, generous towers, nothing that ends the game.
