## Tag: Hard (a real challenge, quick to retry)

- **Tough but fair**: the first moments still teach the game, then the difficulty climbs fast:
  quicker obstacles and enemies, tighter gaps and timings, fewer moves or less time, smarter
  opponents. It always stays possible: a skilled player can win or keep going (check it in a test).
- **Mistakes count**: one hit ends a run (or very few lives), no automatic help, no slow motion.
- **Instant retry**: one tap or key starts again at once, the best score or time beside the
  current one, and the attempts counted ("Attempt 7").
- Where the genre has levels, the later ones get properly hard; where it is endless, the speed and
  the density keep rising past the genre's usual cap.
