## Tag: Sci-fi (space, robots, lasers and neon)

- **The world is science fiction** whatever the genre: space, a station, a future city or an
  alien planet; heroes are pilots, robots or astronauts; enemies are drones, aliens or rogue
  machines; coins become energy cells or crystals.
- **The look**: a dark background (deep space, night) with bright glowing accents (cyan,
  magenta, lime), clean hard-edged shapes, panel lines, blinking lights, stars or a grid floor.
  Effects are lasers, sparks and shield bubbles.
- **The sound**: synth blips, laser zaps, a low hum; no medieval or cartoon sounds.
- **In 3D**: `pack space-kit`, `pack space-station-kit`, `pack modular-space-kit`, `pack
  ultimate-spaceships` or `pack modular-sci-fi-mega-kit`, with a dark sky and coloured lights.
