## What HQ answered on 10-24 (estimates, unchecked: the research confirms or replaces them)

- **His channels fit poorly**: Kids Cars viewers are children; the Satisfying Sims, Particle Factory and Bot League
  viewers watch, they don't make games. Measure it (vidIQ: audience, ages, countries) instead of guessing.
- **Short videos of the product are the best free channel**: "I typed one sentence, here is the game 5 minutes later"
  shows the product in 10 seconds (Shorts, TikTok, Reels, X). `docs/launch/demo-video.md` has the 60-second Short's script.
- **Free launch posts**: Product Hunt, Show HN, Reddit (r/godot, r/gamedev, r/IndieDev, r/SideProject), Indie Hackers,
  AI tool directories (There's An AI For That, Futurepedia, ...).
- **Built in already**: the Free plan's "Made with TypePlay" badge, share pages, the public gallery; share pages in
  search wait for Luka's yes (`docs/parked.md` row 13).
- **Paid, only after the beta gives real conversion numbers**: ads roughly $1-5 per signup for AI tools, so a few hundred
  users ≈ $300-1,500; small game-dev creators ~$50-500 per video.
