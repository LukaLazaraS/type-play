## 1. Rules the code follows
1. **Plan and credits follow money.** `User.plan` and credit grants change only on `transaction.completed` [FACT R-T1: "fully paid and processed"; fee, `subscription_id` and `invoice_number` are only guaranteed then]. Subscription events change status fields, never the plan or the credits.
2. **One lock per user.** Every ledger or billing write runs in one Prisma interactive transaction that first does `SELECT id FROM "User" WHERE id = $1 FOR UPDATE` [FACT R-DB1: Read Committed gives no read-then-write protection; row locks serialise writers].
3. **Integers only.** Credits in tenths, money in cents parsed from Paddle's strings ("2000" = $20.00 [FACT R-C2]); rounding half-up, once per line.
4. **Idempotent by natural keys.** `Payment.id` = `txn_`, `Adjustment.id` = `adj_`, `PaddleEvent.id` = `evt_`, one reversal row per `adj_` (unique). A retry or replay is a no-op insert.
5. **Store, answer 200, then process.** The webhook route verifies the signature, stores the raw event, answers within Paddle's 5 s [FACT R-W1], and a worker applies it with retries.
6. **Pull as the safety net.** After a checkout the page asks the API to fetch the transaction from Paddle if no webhook landed in 20 s; `admin sync` does the same for a user on demand.
7. **Nothing moves money against a person without a person.** A second subscription, an unknown price, a non-USD transaction or a Paddle-initiated refund bigger than the unused share is parked for the admin, never guessed.

## 4. Ledger (`apps/server/src/credits/ledger-math.ts` pure functions; `ledger.service.ts` under the lock)
- **Spend order** [FACT P1]: open, unfrozen `free` buckets (soonest expiry first) → `plan` / `upgrade` by `startsAt` → `pack` by `expiresAt` → `admin` (last, no expiry: ESTIMATE/choice, P1 does not place them). One `charge` row per message (note `<new|edit> on <model>` unchanged) plus allocation rows; a shortfall becomes a null-bucket allocation and `debtTenths`; the next grant settles debt first. `available` = Σ remaining of open unfrozen buckets − debt.
- **Expiry dates**: plan/upgrade bucket = `periodEnd + 1 month` (the carried month; corrected from `billing_period.ends_at` at the renewal); pack = +12 months; free = `freeCycleEndsAt`; admin = none. "A month" = the same day next month, clamped to the month's end; "a day" = UTC [ESTIMATE/choice, as `startOfUtcDay` today].
- **Carry-over cap** at each renewal [FACT P1]: before the new bucket, the other open plan/upgrade buckets are capped at the *new* plan's monthly tenths (the plan just paid for: ESTIMATE/choice), the excess expired oldest first; so a subscriber holds at most two months' worth [ESTIMATE: follows from the rule].
- **Credits for a transaction** = Σ over `details.line_items[]` of `round_half_up(creditsTenths(price) × quantity × rate)`, `rate` = `proration.rate` (null → 1) [FACT R-S3: proration to the minute, the rate is on the line item]; negative lines (the unused old plan on an upgrade) subtract; net ≤ 0 → nothing, parked. Medium→High at half a period: 275×0.5 − 100×0.5 = 87.5 credits for the ~$15 charged [ESTIMATE: arithmetic]. Constant `UPGRADE_CREDITS: "prorated" | "full"` in `credits.ts`; "full" is the loophole in §14(a).
- **Refund quote** = `round_half_up(paidCents × remainingTenths / grantedTenths) − refundedCents`, pre-tax (`tax_mode: "external"`: Paddle adds the tax share [FACT R-A1]); the policy's example (Medium, 30 of 100 used) gives exactly 1400 cents [FACT P2]. Credits removed on approval: the frozen bucket's whole remaining for our own requests; otherwise `min(remaining, round(granted × refundSubtotalCents / paidCents))`.
- **`settle(user)`** (top of every balance read, charge, grant, history, export and admin call, plus a 10-minute sweep over users whose `nextSettleAt` passed; the lock is taken only then, so `GET /me` polling stays read-only): expire due buckets (one `expire` row each: "Free credits ended", "Credits from October ended", "Pack credits ended"); Free users past `freeCycleEndsAt`: close the old free bucket, grant 20 (note "Free plan"; at most one free grant per month per user, so a cancel cannot farm it), next cycle; `past_due` users 24 h past `periodEndsAt`: plan → free, plan buckets expired, packs stay (a recovered renewal restores the plan by money) [ESTIMATE/choice, §7]; recompute `nextSettleAt`.
- **Holds** (High sells two builds at once [FACT P1]; the check runs before the run and the charge minutes after [FACT P3]): `ActiveRun.holdTenths = estimate.high`; `assertCanRun` subtracts the user's active holds from `available` for its own check only (memory only, no contract change; the e2e's three parallel runs on 30 credits still pass: 30, 27.9, 25.8 ≥ 1.2 [ESTIMATE: arithmetic on `credits.ts`]). The daily check stays "todayLeft ≤ 0 before the run".
- **Kill switch** [FACT P1]: a Free run starts with `Message.plan = free`; before one, `SUM(costUsd) WHERE plan = 'free' AND createdAt ≥ UTC midnight` (failed runs included: their cost is stored [FACT P3]) ≥ `FREE_DAILY_AI_USD = 20` → 402 `free_paused`. $20 is read as API list price before VAT; P1's "~280 credits" is the after-VAT reading [ESTIMATE: 20 ÷ 0.06 = 333; 20 ÷ 0.0708 = 282] (§14(b)).
- Wiring: `auth.service.ensureUser` → `grant(free, 20)`; `runs.service.succeed` passes its `tx` so version, message, project and charge stay one transaction (lock the user row first; one lock order everywhere); `admin grant` → `admin` bucket (negative consumes in order, no daily-limit effect); `admin plan` stays entitlement-only [FACT P3 e2e].

## 5. Paddle setup
- Sandbox and live are separate accounts with their own keys, prices and destinations; `https://sandbox-api.paddle.com` / `https://api.paddle.com`; keys `pdl_sdbx_apikey_…` / `pdl_live_apikey_…`, client tokens `test_…` / `live_…` [FACT R-E1, R-E2].
- **Catalog** script `apps/server/scripts/paddle-catalog.ts` (`npm run paddle:catalog -w @typeplay/server -- --env sandbox|live [--tax-category saas|standard]`; idempotent: lists first, creates what is missing): products Medium, High, Ultra, Ultra+, Credit pack (`tax_category: "saas"`; whether it needs approval is contradicted across Paddle's pages [FACT R-C3], the flag covers both); prices USD `"2000"`, `"5000"`, `"10000"`, `"20000"` with `billing_cycle { month, 1 }`, the pack `"1000"` with `billing_cycle: null` (one-time), all `tax_mode: "external"` [FACT P1, R-C2], quantity 1-1 (pack 1-10); each price's `custom_data = { plan, credits }` / `{ pack: true, credits: 40 }`. Prints the five `pri_` ids for the env (§10). At start the API fetches each price: a mismatch with `credits.ts` (amount or `custom_data.credits`) refuses to start; Paddle unreachable → billing endpoints answer 503 until a 5-minute retry succeeds.
- **Customer link**: first checkout → `POST /customers { email, name, custom_data: { userId } }`; on 409 `customer_already_exists` → `GET /customers?email=` [FACT R-C5]; store `User.paddleCustomerId`. Webhooks resolve the user by `custom_data.userId`, then `customer_id`; neither → parked.
- **Checkout**: server-created transactions only: `POST /transactions { items: [{ price_id, quantity: 1 }], customer_id, custom_data: { userId, item, consentVersion, consentAt }, collection_mode: "automatic" }` [FACT R-C6]; the browser runs `Paddle.Checkout.open({ transactionId, settings: { displayMode: "overlay", theme: "light", locale: "en", allowLogout: false } })` [FACT R-J1]. `custom_data` is copied to the subscription and from there to every renewal, upgrade and one-time charge [FACT R-C7], so every later `transaction.completed` names the user. Paddle adds its own California auto-renew box and the save-card consent [FACT R-J3]. A `draft` transaction (no address yet) is "the first stage of a checkout before customer details are captured" [FACT R-C6]; the overlay collects them for a server-created one (sandbox 2026-10-25: email and country prefilled, Continue, then the card).
- **Packs**: the same path with the one-time price (card re-entered unless saved cards are on); "paid plans only" is enforced by our endpoint (403 `plan_required`); a hand-made Paddle checkout still pays real money, so the webhook grants it and logs an anomaly. `POST /subscriptions/{id}/charge` (card on file, no checkout [FACT R-S6]) waits until the sandbox proves the receipt, `custom_data` and the declined-card path.
- **Client** `src/billing/paddle.client.ts`: `fetch`, `Authorization: Bearer`, `Paddle-Version: 1`, 10 s timeout; GETs retried 3×; POST and PATCH never auto-retried (Paddle has no idempotency keys: list or get first [FACT R-E4]). No SDK (its verifier is fixed at 5 s, old-only, non-constant-time [FACT R-W2]).

## 6. Webhooks: `POST /webhooks/paddle` (`@Public()`; the global AuthGuard otherwise demands a token [FACT P3])
- `main.ts`: `NestFactory.create(AppModule, { rawBody: true })`, `app.useBodyParser("json", { limit: "1mb" })`; the handler reads `req.rawBody` [FACT R-N1], never `JSON.stringify(req.body)` [FACT R-W2: "don't transform or process the raw body"].
- **Verify** (`paddle.signature.ts`): `Paddle-Signature: ts=<unix>;h1=<hex>[;h1=…]`; `HMAC-SHA256(secret, "<ts>:<rawBody>")` hex, `timingSafeEqual` against every `h1` for `PADDLE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` and the optional `_PREVIOUS` (rotation = a second destination, two secrets during the switch) [FACT R-W2]; reject `|now − ts| > 300 s` (Paddle's SDK uses 5 s; retries carry a fresh `ts` [FACT R-W6]; the wider window costs nothing because `event_id` dedupe defeats replays: ESTIMATE/choice), log skew > 5 s. Bad signature → 401; bad JSON → 400; database down → 500 (Paddle retries 60× over 3 days live, 3× in 15 min sandbox [FACT R-W1]).
- **Store** `PaddleEvent` with `INSERT … ON CONFLICT (id) DO UPDATE SET deliveries = deliveries + 1, status = CASE WHEN status = 'failed' THEN 'received' ELSE status END` (a replay keeps its `event_id` [FACT R-W4]); answer `{ ok: true }` at once. Subscribed: the 11 events below; anything else → `ignored`. `GET /health` adds `billing: { mode, inbox: { received, failed, parked }, lastEventAt }`.
- **Process** (`inbox.service.ts`): one in-process loop (`setImmediate` after each store, every 30 s, and at start) claims `status = received AND (nextTryAt IS NULL OR nextTryAt <= now) ORDER BY occurredAt FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED LIMIT 50` and runs each handler under the user lock; an error → `attempts++`, `nextTryAt = now + min(60 s × 2^attempts, 1 h)`, 20 attempts → `failed`; unknown user, price or currency → `parked`. Handlers are pure functions of (payload, occurred_at), so the verify pull, `admin sync` and `admin retry` reuse them (synthetic ids `txn:<id>` / `adj:<id>`, deduped by rule 4).

| Event | What we do | Idempotency key |
|---|---|---|
| `transaction.completed` | require USD (else park); user by `custom_data.userId`, then `customer_id`; `Payment` insert (conflict → done); copy consent from `CheckoutSession`; `paymentMethod` from `payments[0].method_details.card`; credits per §4, by `origin` [FACT R-T1]: `web` + plan price → `plan`, `subscriptionStatus active`, `paddleSubscriptionId` (a different active one → flag `two_subscriptions`, no auto-cancel), `periodEndsAt`, bucket `plan`; `subscription_recurring` → settle, cap, bucket `plan`, plan from the price (a pending downgrade lands here), clear `pendingPlan`, status `active`; `subscription_update` → bucket `upgrade` (expires with the period's plan bucket), plan from the positive line; `web` + pack price or `subscription_charge` → bucket `pack` (+12 months); `subscription_payment_method_change` → nothing | `Payment.id` = `txn_` |
| `transaction.payment_failed` | `User.lastPaymentFailedAt = max(occurred_at)` (the banner only) | `occurred_at` |
| `subscription.created` `.updated` `.activated` `.past_due` `.paused` `.resumed` `.canceled` | one `applySubscription(entity, occurred_at)` (each carries the full entity [FACT R-S1]): skip if `occurred_at ≤ User.subscriptionUpdatedAt` (a change applied straight from Paddle's answer, i.e. plan change, cancel, keep, sync, is ordered by the entity's `updated_at`: Paddle's clock, never ours; a PC running 32 s slow skipped a cancel, sandbox 2026-10-25); set `paddleCustomerId` (if empty), `paddleSubscriptionId`, `subscriptionStatus`, `nextBilledAt`, `periodEndsAt = current_billing_period?.ends_at`, `cancelsAt = scheduled_change.action == "cancel" ? effective_at : null`; `canceled` and `paused` → plan `free`, every plan/upgrade bucket expired ("when your plan ends, its monthly credits end with it" [FACT P2]), packs stay, free cycle started; `past_due` → status only; never set the plan from `items` | `User.subscriptionUpdatedAt` |
| `adjustment.created`, `adjustment.updated` | upsert `Adjustment` (max `occurred_at`; the two can arrive in either order [FACT R-A2]); `approved` and no reversal yet → reverse credits (§4), `Payment.refundedCents +=`, status, resolve the `Refund` row or flag `refund_review`; `rejected` → unfreeze; `chargeback` / `chargeback_warning` → reverse the payment's remaining credits, `charged_back`, flag `chargeback` (runs and checkouts refused 403 `billing_hold` until an admin clears it: the account owes the sale and a $20 fee [FACT R-A4]); `chargeback_reverse` → logged, restored by hand | `CreditEntry.paddleAdjustmentId` |

## 7. Flows
- **New plan**: `/pricing` → signed in? `/billing/checkout?item=plan:medium` (else sign in, then there) → summary, tick box, acceptance line (§8) → `POST /billing/checkout` → overlay → `checkout.completed` (a UI event; `data.transaction_id` is the `txn_` [FACT R-J2]) → the page polls `GET /billing/checkout/:txn` every 2 s; at 20 s `POST …/verify` pulls `GET /transactions/{id}` and applies it through the same handler → "You're on Medium. 100 credits added." Webhook or pull, the first wins; the other is a no-op.
- **Renewal**: `transaction.completed` origin `subscription_recurring` → settle → cap → grant → plan from the price.
- **Upgrade**: `GET /billing/plan-change?plan=high` previews with `PATCH …/preview` `prorated_immediately` → one line "You pay $14.00 now plus tax, charged to Visa ending 4242, and get about 82 credits now; then $50 a month from 17 Nov" (the card on file is charged with no Paddle checkout, so the amount is shown before the button: ESTIMATE, legal reading of California §17602(a) [R-L13]) → `POST` → `PATCH` → Paddle bills (origin `subscription_update`) → `transaction.completed` grants. Its lines (sandbox 2026-10-25): the new plan prorated, and the old plan's unused share as a line of quantity −1 with negative totals; credits = new × rate − old × rate, the minus counted once (Medium → High on day 1: 174.9). A declined card → Paddle refuses the change (`prevent_change` [FACT R-S2]) → "Your card was declined. Update it in Manage billing." Guards: active only (not `past_due`, not within 30 minutes of `nextBilledAt` [FACT R-S2]), no `pendingPlan` (409 `billing_busy`: "Undo your change to Medium first").
- **Downgrade at renewal** (P1 promises it; Paddle cannot schedule an item change: `scheduled_change` holds only cancel/pause/resume [FACT R-S2]): preview with `do_not_bill`; refuse unless `immediate_transaction` is null and `next_transaction`'s subtotal equals the new plan's price [FACT R-S4 fields; `do_not_bill` = "isn't billed for the prorated amount or the full amount", while `full_next_billing_period` bills the full amount at the next renewal [FACT R-S3], which both judges flagged]; then `PATCH … do_not_bill`, `User.pendingPlan = plan`; nothing else changes until the renewal's `transaction.completed` carries the new price. Undo: `DELETE /billing/plan-change` (PATCH back, `do_not_bill`). The preview (sandbox 2026-10-25): nothing now, exactly the new price at the renewal. **To confirm**: whether Paddle's "Subscription Price Changed" email goes out on the PATCH [R-M1] (sandbox mail reaches only the account's own domain).
- **Cancel**: `POST /billing/cancel` → `POST /subscriptions/{id}/cancel` (period end by default [FACT R-S5]) → `subscription.updated` with `scheduled_change` → `cancelsAt`; "Keep my plan" → `PATCH { scheduled_change: null }`. The portal's links stay next to it (invoices, card, the EU "Request withdrawal" button [FACT R-L4]).
- **Past due**: features and already-granted credits stay while Paddle retries (7× over 30 days, then cancels [FACT R-S7]); no new credits; 24 h after `periodEndsAt` the settle sweep drops the plan to free (§4) [ESTIMATE/choice: stops 30 days of free exports; a recovered renewal restores the plan by money].
- **Refund** (`admin refund <email> <txn|last> [reason]`, asked by email as the refund page says [FACT P2]): under the lock: within 14 days, not refunded, no pending `Refund`, bucket open, **no active run** (409 `billing_busy`: closes the charge-vs-refund race) → quote ($0 → refused) → freeze the bucket → `POST /adjustments { action: "refund", type: "partial", transaction_id, tax_mode: "external", reason, items: [{ item_id, type: "partial", amount }] }` [FACT R-A1] → `Refund` pending | approved → the webhook finishes it. A timeout → `lookup`; a 5-minute job finds it with `GET /adjustments?transaction_id=` before ever posting again (Paddle refuses a second pending refund per transaction [FACT R-A1]). Sandbox approves every 10 min; live auto-approves ≤ $400 on a verified account [FACT R-A3]. A refund approved while a build runs (Paddle-initiated, can't be blocked): reversal first, charge after, both under the lock.
- **Account close** (`DELETE /me`): cancel the subscription at Paddle `immediately` first (refuse with "Try again in an hour" inside Paddle's 30-minute lock [FACT R-S5] or when Paddle is unreachable), then delete; `Payment`, `Adjustment`, `PaddleEvent` keep their rows with `userId = null` (cards are refundable for 120 days, PayPal 179 [FACT R-A5]; the privacy policy keeps "records we must keep by law" [FACT P2]). A later event for that customer is `ignored`.
