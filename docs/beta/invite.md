# Beta 1: the invitation

For Luka. Make a beta code first (`npm run admin:live -w @typeplay/server -- code add --uses 20 --credits 50 "Beta 1"`,
`docs/beta/runbook.md` step 8) and put its six digits and credits into the text below; one code serves the whole group.
Or invite someone by address (`invite add <their Google address>`) and use the "by address" line in step 2 instead.
Before you send: put in their name; keep the Smart and Best line only if you moved them to a paid plan with
`admin plan`. The short version for a YouTube community post is at the end.

---

**Subject:** You're invited to try TypePlay first

Hi [name],

I'm building **TypePlay**: you type the game you want in one sentence, and an AI builds it in a few minutes. You play it
right away in your browser, and you change it just by writing what you want different.

I'd love you to try it in the first closed beta, before it opens to everyone. **It's free**: you start with [20 + the
code's credits, e.g. 70] credits, and I add more by hand when you need them. No card, nothing to pay. A new game costs
about 2 credits and a change about 0.3. [Keep only if they're on a paid plan: You can also pick Smart or Best, which make better games and cost more
credits. The cost is shown before you press Build.]

**How to start**

1. Open **https://typeplay.app/create** on a computer or a phone.
2. Press **Sign in with Google** (any Google account), then type your beta code: **[the six digits]**.
   [By address instead: use this Google account: **[the address you invited]**; with another one you'll see a "closed
   beta" page, which has a button to switch accounts.]
3. Type your idea and press **Build my game**. It takes a few minutes; you can leave the page and come back.

**What to try**

- **Make a game.** Runners and puzzle games work best right now, for example: "Make an endless runner where a fox jumps
  over logs at night", or "Make a match-3 puzzle with cute fruit".
- **Change it by chat:** "make the fox faster", "add coins", "make it night". Every change is a new version, and you can
  go back to an older one.
- **Play it on your phone:** open the game on your phone (sign in there too), or press **Share** and open the link.
- **Show a friend** with the share link, if you like.

**Feedback: the most useful part.** Press **Send feedback**: it's in the menu under your picture (top right), and in
**More** on a game's page. Write what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. On a game's page you can
attach the game, so I can play the exact version you saw. Small things count too: a word you didn't understand, a
button you couldn't find, a game that came out boring. You can send up to 10 notes a day; for anything longer, or a
screenshot, write to me.

**Private by default.** Your games and the sentences you type are private unless you press Share. The games are yours.

**How long:** the beta runs for about two weeks from Friday 23 October. Then TypePlay gets paid plans, and later it
opens to everyone. Your account and your games stay.

Questions, or something broken? Write to **hello@typeplay.app** or message me on **+995 598 398 198**.

Thank you for helping!
Luka

## Short version: a YouTube community post (3 lines)

> I'm building TypePlay: type a game idea in one sentence, an AI builds it, and you play it in your browser minutes later.
> A small closed beta starts Friday 23 October: free for testers, and you change your game just by chatting with it.
> Want to test it? Email hello@typeplay.app with the Google address you'd sign in with: I'm picking 10 testers.

The post brings requests to hello@typeplay.app (it forwards to Luka's Gmail); invite the ones you pick with `admin invite
add`, then send them the invitation above. Nobody gets in from the post alone: until they're invited, signing in shows the
closed-beta page.
