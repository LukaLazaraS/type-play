## The mail

One mail per person, from Luka's Gmail (the project's only sending path: `hello@typeplay.app` is Cloudflare Email
Routing, receive only, and forwards to that Gmail; `docs/cloudflare.md` §1), plain text, no one else's address on it.
`{first name}` is the first word of the name Google gives for the account ("Hi there" when it isn't a name);
`{day}` is the first sign-in's day, written "4 November"; `{address}` is the account's own address. Sent one by one,
a minute or two apart, the Outlook and Hotmail addresses last (the design review, 2026-11-19).

**Subject:** You're in: make your first game on TypePlay

> Hi {first name},
>

> is open now, on the free plan.

> browser. Then you change it by writing what you want: "make the fox faster", "add coins", "make it night".

> Start here: https://typeplay.app/create
> Sign in with the same Google account ({address}).

>   about one small change that day; the next day brings 5 more. About 4 new games a month.
> - A new game takes 10 to 25 minutes. You can close the tab: it keeps building, and it waits for you under My games, at the top right.
> - Simple ideas work best on the free plan, for example "An endless runner where a fox jumps over logs at night".
