## Decisions (taken tonight, the standard way; Luka can change any)

- **Upload first, send with the message** (the way ChatGPT, Claude.ai and Slack do it): each picture uploads as it is
  attached (`POST /attachments`, the bytes as the body), so a bad file is refused at once and Send is instant; the message
  then names the uploaded ids (`images` in `POST /projects` and `POST /projects/:id/messages`). An upload not sent within
  24 hours is deleted by a sweep.
- **Where they live:** the API's data folder, `data/attachments/<2-char shard>/<id>.*` (the game's other private data, its
  folder and history, lives in `data/projects/`; R2 holds only public builds and Android files). Never in the game
  folder: Godot would pack anything there into the build. A row per picture (`Attachment`: owner, message, place,
  format, size); the rows go with their message, game and account (cascade), the files with them in the same code
  paths, plus the sweep for anything left (a crash).
- **Three files per picture**, made at upload by sharp (libvips, the Node standard): the clean picture (decoded,
  turned by its EXIF orientation, every metadata block dropped, re-encoded in its own format, at most 2048 px on its long
  edge: a game background never needs more, and a 10 MB PNG would bloat the web build); what the AI sees (WebP, at most
  1568 px and 1.15 megapixels: ~1,500 image tokens, so 10 pictures stay ~15K tokens; the 5.5 models take up to 2576 px /
  4,784 tokens each, but Haiku costs 5x past 100K input); a 320 px thumbnail for the chat.
- **Checked by content:** the first bytes must be PNG, JPEG or WebP, then a full decode (sharp `failOn: warning`), at most
  50 megapixels (decompression bombs), and the decoded format must match the bytes. At most 2 pictures processed at once
  (the live API container has 1 vCPU and 1 GB).
- **The agent sees the pictures in its first message** (Agent SDK streaming input, image blocks before the request;
  probed on this PC: Haiku answered "Red, blue" for a red/blue test picture, $0.0003), and gets each picture's file path to
  copy into the game when the player asks for that. The files reach run.mjs as `--images <dir>`: on this PC a private
  folder per run under `data/run-images/`, deleted after the run; on the live server the runner copies them from
  `/data/attachments` (the API's, 0700) into the job's home (0700 folder, 0600 files, the job's own user, wiped after the
  job), like the prompt file.
- **A picture sent again** (the size check's "Use Smart for this" tap, Try again after a failed first build) is copied
  for the new message, so each message owns its pictures.
- **Limits per account:** at most 30 pictures uploaded and not sent at once, 120 uploads an hour (429, said plainly):
  decoding costs the live API's one vCPU. An upload never sent is deleted after a day.
- **Privacy:** the "What we collect" sentence, and Anthropic's line under "Who else sees it" now names the pictures (the AI
  sees them: leaving it out would be untrue); the legal pages' date moved to 4 November. A provisional call in
  `docs/parked.md` for Luka's website review, as the 10-29 notifications sentence was.
- **The live path tried for real:** the runner (root only, Linux) ran in WSL with a portable Node in `/tmp/node-test`
  and a fake run.mjs (`apps/server/test/runner-pictures.mjs`, 15 checks), since nothing else checks the runner before a
  deploy.
- **The screens (impeccable shape; no questions tonight, so its interview took HQ's brief as the answers):** Operate mode,
  inside DESIGN.md's world. The add control is a round icon button (Phosphor image mark, "Add pictures") at the end of the
  words inside the Field box, the way chat boxes do it (ChatGPT, Claude, WhatsApp), in the composer and in /create's idea
  box alike; Send stays the one hot action. Attached pictures sit in the box above the words as square tiles at the
  notice thumbnail's 12px radius, each with a small ink "Remove" circle; an upload in progress dims its tile with the
  breathing dot. Paste (Ctrl+V, a phone's paste) and dropping files on the composer or the idea box add them; while files
  are dragged over, the box wears the focus ring. In the chat the player's pictures sit above their orange bubble,
  right-aligned, no bubble around them; a tap opens the viewer: the picture whole on ink, "2 of 5", previous and next
  (and the arrow keys), Close (and Escape), the keyboard back on the tile after. Refusals are the hot-dot error line with
  the API's sentence (and the file's name when several came).
