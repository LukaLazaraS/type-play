## Decisions made on the way

- Research notes so far (13:30): Lovable docs (collaboration, share-project, people, workspace, drafts, chat) say
  collaborators use the project owner's workspace credits; Replit's invite page says "their work counts toward your
  credits"; Bolt says the prompter's own tokens (the opposite). Raw pages saved in the job's tmp/research folder.
- **CLEAR**: Lovable and Replit agree on all three gate questions (the owner pays; email or link + account + accept;
  owner + editor). Bolt, v0 and Figma bill the sender: noted in the research, Luka asked for Lovable and Replit.
- Roles: owner + editor only (both sell a viewer role on paid tiers only: later). Where they differ the stricter way:
  editors can't share publicly / gallery (Replit's Editor can't publish; Lovable's and Bolt's can), can't invite or
  remove (Replit and Bolt: the owner manages access; the owner pays), can't do Android (the upload key is the owner's
  store identity), can't delete. Editors may rename, restore, download, stop their own builds.
- Invitations: bound to one email (Bolt), 7 days (Lovable link, Replit email), a new link replaces the old (Lovable).
  No open "anyone with the link can edit" link (Bolt removed it; owner-pays makes it risky).
- We have no email-sending service (hello@ only receives): the owner sends the link themselves; an invitee with an
  account also sees it in My games. Sending service = For Luka.
- Plan limits: Lovable's way (every plan may invite: we sell credits, not seats); 10 people per game besides the owner
  (invitations waiting included); at most 30 new links a day per owner (a guard: we send no email, so no spam vector).
- Questions the AI asked: only the person who asked answers them; another member's message is a new request.
- Removing / leaving / closing the account stops a build that person asked for that is still going or waiting
  (nothing charged), as the access is gone.
- Old messages: the migration fills `Message.authorId` with the game's owner; null later = a former member.
- The owner's balance never reaches an editor (Lovable shows it read-only; we show less): the editor sees prices and
  "about 0.3 credits · <owner> pays".
- The review's two agents disagreed on My games' second hot button (A: make New game quiet while invitations wait; B:
  make Accept quiet). Took B: DESIGN.md's "one hot action per view" holds with any number of invitations (A's way
  would make each Accept hot), and the invitation's own page keeps its hot Accept.
- An accepted invitation's row is kept while its person is in the game (so the link opens the game, the critique's
  P1) and deleted the moment they're out (removed, left, closed account), so no address of someone gone is kept.
- The owner may stop a member's build (the owner pays), after an inline question that keeps the keyboard on "Keep
  building"; a member stops only their own.
- The old e2e's one failure tonight (`write ECONNABORTED` in the closed-beta checks, before any pair-developing check)
  is a socket the test reused after a blocking admin command; its retry covers ECONNRESET only. Pre-existing, not
