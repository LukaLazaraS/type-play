those don't: website and app builders re-checked, refund policies, Paddle's rules and fees,
consumer law, and currency practice. "[3rd party]" = not from the vendor's own pages.

## 3. Paddle: rules and fees

- **Review:** Terms, Refund and Privacy must be reachable from the site's navigation; no refund
  window is required [paddle.com/help … what-is-domain-verification]. The seller handbook
  "requires" buyer support details "(email and phone number)", acceptance of terms and refund
  policy before purchase, notice to Paddle of policy changes, and the merchant-of-record
  sentence; it *recommends* a 30-day money-back guarantee [paddle.com/seller-guides/seller-handbook].
  One founder's rejection: "Refund policy contains qualifiers. Please provide unconditional refund
  terms matching our standard policy" [dev.to/pavelbuild, single report]. Paddle sellers with
  usage-based rules exist (Prismfy, Eventplot, Hesse.ai).
- **Paddle's own refund policy** (31 Mar 2026): non-refundable unless the law requires (§1.1);
  Paddle "may, at its sole discretion, issue a refund if a request is submitted within 14 days"
  (§7.1), weighing usage (§7.2); statutory rights override any seller policy (§2.1)
  [paddle.com/legal/refund-policy]. Seller terms: we may not refund buyers directly (§10.1); Paddle
  may refund within 14 days and we owe the refund plus fees (§10.2, §10.4); high refund or
  chargeback rates can mean suspension (§10.3) [paddle.com/legal/terms].
- **Credits:** prohibited list bans "virtual currency or stored value … store credit, gift cards,
  vouchers" [paddle.com/help … what-am-i-not-allowed-to-sell]; the AI-companies page says to "sell
  prepaid credits as one-time products and track the balance in your app"
  [developer.paddle.com/get-started/how-paddle-works/ai-companies]. Native usage billing is a
  beta by application. So we keep the ledger.
- **Fees:** 5% + $0.50 on the total including tax, no monthly fee; under $10 "contact us for
  bespoke pricing" [paddle.com/pricing]; Paddle keeps its fee on a refund (`retained_fee`);
  chargeback or pre-chargeback alert $20, never returned, keep chargebacks under 0.65%
  [paddle.com/help … understanding-chargebacks]; conversion into the payout currency mid-market
  + 2% (USD/EUR/GBP); payouts monthly by the 15th from $100; a wire outside the bank's local
  currency $15 SWIFT plus up to 1.5% conversion; Payoneer: no Paddle fee, Payoneer's own fees
  [paddle.com/help … is-there-a-fee-taken-for-payouts].
- **Subscriptions:** proration modes per change (`prorated_immediately` etc.); cancel at period
  end by default; partial refunds by adjustment; Paddle sends renewal reminders only for plans of
  6 months or longer, and only where the law requires; a California auto-renewal consent box on
  every checkout since 1 Jul 2025 [developer.paddle.com].
- **Currencies:** automatic conversion into 30 currencies (prices come out unrounded, e.g. €9.38
  for $10) or up to 250 fixed price overrides per country; override first, then conversion, then
  the base price [developer.paddle.com/build/products/offer-localized-pricing].

## 4. Consumer law, the parts that matter

- **EU:** CJEU *Sky Österreich*, C‑234/25 (9 July 2026): a "dynamic" offering is a digital
  *service*, so the digital-content exception (Art. 16(m)) doesn't apply; a consumer who asked for
  the service to start pays "an amount which is in proportion to what has been provided"
  (Art. 14(3)), and the right ends once the service is fully performed with prior express consent
  and acknowledgement (Art. 16(a)). Without the express request the consumer owes nothing
  (Art. 14(4)). Since 19 Jun 2026 a "withdraw from contract here" function is required
  (Directive 2023/2673); Paddle's customer portal has one [eur-lex.europa.eu CELEX 62025CJ0234].
- **UK:** the DMCC Act subscription rules start in **January 2027** (gov.uk, 9 Aug 2026):
  cooling-off at the start and after a 12-month+ renewal, reminders at least every 6 months, easy
  online exit. Today the existing 14-day cooling-off applies.
- **US:** the FTC "click to cancel" rule was vacated (8th Cir., Jul 2025), rulemaking restarted
  (ANPRM 13 Mar 2026); ROSCA still applies. California (since 1 Jul 2025): consent to the renewal
  terms, online cancel at will, price-change notice, an annual reminder, consent records 3 years
  [california.public.law … 17602].

## 5. Currencies elsewhere

₹2,399 a month, GST included; some users still see $20) [techcrunch.com/2026/07/13]. ChatGPT:
