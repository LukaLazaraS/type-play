## Step 1 of 2: the core game

This session builds only the playable core of what the player asked for: the rules,
controls (keyboard + touch), title overlay, score or goal, game over / win and restart, and
the difficulty curve, with clean simple visuals (one palette, tidy shapes). Leave out the
extras: particles, screen shake, sounds, and pickups or modes the player didn't ask for. A
second session adds the polish. Run the check, look at the screenshots, and write NOTES.md,
ending with a short "Polish ideas" list for step 2.
