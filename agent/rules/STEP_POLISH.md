## Step 2 of 2: polish

The core game already works and is saved; NOTES.md describes it. Read NOTES.md first, then
only the scripts you need. Make it look and feel great without changing its rules or
controls: art and animation (character, background layers), juice (tweens, particles, screen
shake, squash and stretch), sounds generated in code, a nicer title and game-over screen, and
the genre patterns that fit. Change files with small edits rather than rewrites, run the
check, look at the screenshots, and update NOTES.md.
